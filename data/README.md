# data — the small derived tables the paper quotes

Filled from the final run of the modules (copies of `NN_name/data/*.tsv` that are small enough
for git): the DMP and DMR tables with their annotation, the label swap counts, the gene level
decoupling table and its summary, the module assignments and DMP enrichment tables (all three
stratifications), the promoter Weber classification of *D. laeve*, human and the four
comparison species, the JASPAR to *D. laeve* motif bridge (`jaspar_ortholog_bridge.tsv`) and
the TF guide (`tf_dlaeve_guide.tsv`), the HOMER known motif tables, the LMR methylation change
and bin QC tables, and the TE statistics. Per CpG tables (25 million rows) and R objects are not
here; they are regenerated by the modules and the per CpG methylation is deposited with the
raw data.
